## AuctionHouse — Player Guide

A player-to-player item trading system. No economy, no signs — just list, offer, claim.

---

### Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/ah` | Open the shop |
| `/ah sell` | List an item for trade |
| `/ah search <item>` | Search by item name |
| `/ah my` | View your listings |
| `/ah claim` | Collect your pending items |

---

### How to Sell

1. Type `/ah sell`
2. Place items in the **top row** and click **Confirm**
3. Your listing is now live — other players can submit offers

### How to Buy

1. Click any listing in the shop
2. Place your offer items in the deposit strip and click **Confirm**
3. Wait for the seller to accept or reject

### Handling Offers

- Go to **My Listings** and click your listing to see offers
- **Accept** — both players get each other's items through claims
- **Reject** — the offer is removed

### Claims

Items from accepted, rejected, or expired listings go to your **Claims**. Run `/ah claim` and click to withdraw, or hit **Collect All** to grab everything at once.

---

### Search and Sort

- Click the **sign** button and type in chat to filter listings
- Click the **barrier** button to clear the search
- Click the **compass** to toggle newest / oldest order

### Expiry

Listings expire after a set time and return to the owner's claims. Refresh the page if you see stale listings.

---

### Limits

- **5** active listings per player
- **54** items max per offer
- **10** pending offers per player

### Tips

- Check your claims — unclaimed items aren't in your inventory yet
- You cannot offer on your own listing
- Make sure you have inventory space before claiming
